Processing pipeline five steps, run in ~1 s on 30 s of 8 kHz audio

Heavy stages are chunked across animation frames, so the interface never freezes. All intermediate products are shown below as they are produced.

1

Noise rejection — blind source separation

Simulation and stereo input give two microphone channels; FastICA whitens and deflates them into independent sources, then the component carrying 200–1200 Hz breath-band energy is kept. Single-channel input falls back to spectral gating with a per-bin noise floor.

idle
2

Wavelet analysis — Morlet CWT, breath isolation, cycle detection

A continuous Morlet transform (16 log-spaced scales, 150–1500 Hz) localises energy in time–frequency. Band-passing 200–1200 Hz and taking the rectified, smoothed energy yields a breathing envelope at 40 Hz; cycles are found as envelope peaks at least 1.6 s apart.

idle
3

Takens delay embedding — τ by AMI, m by false nearest neighbours

τ is the first minimum of average mutual information (the delay at which the signal stops explaining itself). m is the dimension at which the fraction of false nearest neighbours drops below 5%. The envelope is then unfolded into that space.

idle
4

Invariants — correlation dimension, Lyapunov exponent, entropy

Grassberger–Procaccia: the slope of log C(r) vs log r gives D₂ over the scaling region. Rosenstein: the initial slope of the mean logarithmic divergence between neighbouring trajectories gives λ₁. Sample entropy is computed on the cycle-aligned residual, so phase drift cannot masquerade as complexity.

idle
5

Risk logic — ADI against the personal baseline

The three invariants are scaled by cohort-calibrated spans and combined into one 0–100 Airway Dynamics Index. ADI is compared with the mean and spread of the patient's own baseline nights; Watch and Early Warning are raised on cohort-floored thresholds.
